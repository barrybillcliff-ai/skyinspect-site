Built from the roof up

Roofing experience first. Better inspection technology second.

Sky Inspect is being built by a working New Hampshire roofer through Merrimack Valley Roofing—grounded in real roof conditions, FAA Part 107 operations and practical autonomous capture workflows.

Made for the people who must act on the report.

A roof inspection is useful only when its evidence helps someone make a safer, faster decision. Our approach starts with field reality: consistent coverage, clear imagery, traceable locations and honest limits.

Autonomous survey grids improve repeatability. Geotagged photos keep findings tied to place. AI-assisted OK, Review and Urgent flags help qualified people prioritize attention—they do not replace adjusters, engineers, code officials or hands-on investigation when required.

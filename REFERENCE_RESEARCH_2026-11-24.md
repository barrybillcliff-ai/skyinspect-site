# Sky Inspect reference-site research

Research date: 2026-11-24. The audit combined rendered-page inspection, DOM/CSS motion counts, content structure, responsive navigation inspection, and public page text. Motion counts are visible elements with a CSS transition or named animation at the sampled desktop viewport; they are comparative indicators, not claims about hand-authored animation sequences.

## SkySpec AI

- Visual language: near-black background, electric blue accent, glassy hero panel, white heavy Inter typography, technical mono styling for metrics.
- Media: 19 visible photographs, no visible hero video or canvas/3D scene in the sampled state.
- Motion: 52 visible transitioned elements; primarily hover/background/color/opacity micro-interactions. No named keyframe animation appeared in the sample.
- Homepage order: sticky navigation; dark photographic hero with two CTAs; four-metric proof strip; service-explainer blocks; four-step process; AI module/capabilities; closing CTA.
- CTAs/forms: waitlist as the persistent high-intent CTA, paired with early-access/demo CTAs; no homepage form.
- Trust: model-precision, speed, geographic readiness and AI-engine metrics; detailed deliverables reinforce technical credibility.
- Mobile: standard collapsing navigation, stacked CTAs/cards, large type reduced but retained; imagery supports rather than replaces copy.
- Best idea used: dark technical credibility, compact status/proof strip, detailed capture-to-report explanation.

## DroneDeploy

- Visual language: enterprise editorial photography, black/white foundation, bright yellow and campaign blue accents, oversized Hanken Grotesk headlines, UI overlays on field imagery.
- Media: 51 visible images, 13 SVGs, no visible hero video at the sample state.
- Motion: 59 transitioned/animated elements; named banner animation plus a scroll animation; image/UI layers and button transforms create product-story motion.
- Homepage order: announcement bar; deep enterprise nav; full-width field hero; customer-scale/logo proof; value pillars; use-case/customer-story modules; learning content; demo CTA.
- CTAs/forms: repeated Book a demo CTA plus lower-friction content exploration; conversion is sales-led rather than form-heavy on the homepage.
- Trust: “3 million sites in 180 countries,” customer stories, recognizable enterprise use cases and operational ROI language.
- Mobile: mega-nav becomes a menu; hero content and media stack; CTA buttons remain large and touch-friendly; decorative overlays simplify.
- Best idea used: full-width photographic storytelling, simple outcome language, strong primary/secondary CTA hierarchy.

## Roofr

- Visual language: bright pale-blue canvas, bold black/blue Circular typography, friendly 3D/isometric roof illustration, rounded controls, practical product screenshots.
- Media: 28 visible images and 21 SVGs.
- Motion: 115 visible transitioned elements; extensive hover states, transform/opacity reveals and micro-interactions; no visible hero video.
- Homepage order: promo strip; simple product nav; conversion hero with inline email capture; featured partner proof; product benefits; workflow modules; customer result; pricing/get-started paths.
- CTAs/forms: “Start for free” repeated in nav and page; email field is directly in the hero; two visible forms / 18 inputs in the sampled DOM.
- Trust: partner strip, specific customer revenue result, tool-by-tool practicality and no-credit-card friction reducer.
- Mobile: hero becomes one column, email CTA stacks, 3D artwork scales/crops, product navigation collapses.
- Best idea used: concrete benefit copy, fast path to conversion, friendly card system and explicit pricing preview.

## EagleView

- Visual language: premium black/charcoal hero, restrained forest green/blue, gray-white content fields, Sofia Sans/Lato typography, cinematic aerial media.
- Media: 45 visible images, 17 SVGs and one visible video.
- Motion: 121 visible transitioned/animated elements; a named statistics marquee plus hover/opacity/transform behaviors.
- Homepage order: announcement; enterprise navigation/search; cinematic hero; precision/performance/value pillars; product feature; proof metrics and logo marquee; industry tabs; trusted-leader logos; educational content; final conversion.
- CTAs/forms: paired Explore/Discover or Talk/Buy actions address both research and transaction intent; five forms / 51 inputs present across loaded components.
- Trust: 25+ years, 3.5B images, 98%+ measurement accuracy, top-insurer adoption, savings metric, extensive integration/customer logos.
- Mobile: dense navigation collapses, video remains atmospheric, metrics and industry tabs become stacked/scrollable, trust content is preserved.
- Best idea used: premium restraint, quantified proof, cross-industry grid and credibility before conversion.

## Aerially.ai roof inspection

- Visual language: direct service-page treatment, strong headline, service photography, certification badges and numbered deliverables; light content sections with clear contrast.
- Media/style: photography-first, not 3D; roof/thermal imagery supports specific deliverables.
- Motion: the public text crawl and current browser route did not expose reliable motion counts (the browser route redirected to a registrar lander). The accessible service page indicates a conventional WordPress-style service layout where motion is secondary to clarity.
- Page order: service breadcrumb/hero; headline and quote CTA; certification/price strip; benefit bullets; problem; solution; five deliverables; applications; FAQ; quote form.
- CTAs/forms: phone and “Get free quote” repeat; final form asks for name, email, phone, address and building details; reassurance says free assessment/no obligation.
- Trust: PE-certified, FAA Part 107, SB-4D, starting price, camera/thermal specifications and fast delivery claims.
- Mobile: service navigation collapses and content naturally stacks; repeated phone/quote actions remain visible.
- Best idea used: service-first problem/solution flow, numbered deliverables, plain-language FAQ and conversion form.

## Synthesis for Sky Inspect

Sky Inspect uses a dark navy/charcoal system with bright teal and small safety-orange/red status accents. The hero combines original New England drone photography with an understated survey overlay. The structure follows service intent first: safety problem, four-part solution, three-step operating model, eight-industry grid, transparent pricing preview, pilot proof placeholder, FAQ and final CTA. Motion is intentionally subtler than the most animated references: intersection reveals, card lifts, sticky blurred navigation and accordion micro-interactions, with reduced-motion support. Enterprise credibility comes from explaining limitations honestly—AI flags assist qualified reviewers rather than replacing them—while conversion stays practical through persistent pilot and refundable-preorder paths.

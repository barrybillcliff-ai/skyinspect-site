Pilot fit questions

Answers before we schedule the first roof.

These are the first questions we answer in the pilot-fit review so the test reflects a real production workflow instead of a demo.

Q1
Who is the free pilot for?
The free pilot is for qualified organizations that want to evaluate a repeatable roof-inspection workflow for estimating, insurance, property operations, acquisitions, or portfolio oversight.
Q2
What happens after I submit the pilot form?
Sky Inspect reviews fit, confirms location, roof count, airspace, weather, and scheduling constraints, then follows up to define the pilot-fit review before any flight is booked.
Q3
What do I receive from a successful pilot?
Qualified pilot customers receive a roof inspection output that helps them review imagery, triage conditions, and decide whether the workflow is useful for broader deployment.

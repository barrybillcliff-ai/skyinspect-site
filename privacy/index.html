Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sky Inspect collects and uses information submitted through skyinspect.ai.

Information we collect

We collect the information you provide in pilot, preorder and contact forms, including name, company, role, email, phone number, roof count and service interests. Standard web-server logs may record IP address, browser, timestamp and requested pages.

How we use it

We use information to respond to requests, evaluate pilot eligibility, prepare quotes, administer reservations, provide service and improve our offerings. If you consent, we may contact you by email, phone or text, including through automated technology where permitted.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with service providers that support hosting, communications, payments or operations, and when required by law. We retain information only as reasonably needed for these purposes and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may opt out of marketing email, reply STOP to eligible text messages, or request access, correction or deletion by emailing admin@skyinspect.ai. Consent to automated contact is not a condition of purchase.

Security and children

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet system is perfectly secure. The site is intended for business users and not children under 13.

FAQ

Questions before takeoff.

Is the AI making a final roofing or insurance decision?

No. Sky Inspect provides AI-assisted condition flags to help qualified people prioritize review. It is not a substitute for engineering judgment, coverage determination, code review or a hands-on inspection when one is required.

What does a residential inspection cost?

Typical residential service is expected to range from $125 to $199 depending on roof size, complexity, airspace, travel and deliverables. We confirm price before scheduling.

Can you inspect every roof?

Most roofs can be surveyed, but flights depend on weather, airspace authorization, property access, local restrictions and safe operating conditions.

What do OK, Review and Urgent mean?

They are triage labels. OK means no obvious issue was flagged in the reviewed imagery; Review means a qualified person should examine the evidence; Urgent means the imagery appears to warrant prompt human attention. They are not warranties or final diagnoses.

Do you operate the drone?

Yes. Inspection-as-a-service is the primary offering. Hardware reservations are secondary for organizations planning their own compatible program.

How fast is delivery?

Turnaround depends on scope and demand. A target delivery time is confirmed with every quote, with expedited options considered for storm response.
